# 01 — Business Task

## Background

As part of my data analytics course, I was required to complete a capstone project to demonstrate the skills I had developed.

I have been using Trading 212 to invest since I was 18, but I had never carried out a detailed analysis of my portfolio. I decided to use this project as an opportunity to investigate my own investments using the data analysis techniques I had learned.

I also wanted to apply concepts from my university mathematics degree to a practical financial problem and develop my understanding of quantitative financial risk analysis.

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project was to assess the historical risk profile of my Stocks & Shares ISA portfolio.

My portfolio consists of exchange-traded funds (ETFs) alongside individual stocks, including companies that are already represented within those ETFs.

I wanted to understand how this combination of investments affected my portfolio's exposure to risk, and how its historical risk and performance compared with a simpler strategy of buying and holding an S&P 500 ETF.

## Key Questions

The analysis was guided by four main questions:

1. What is the historical risk profile of my portfolio, and how can it be quantified?
2. Which holdings contribute most to the portfolio's overall risk?
3. How does the portfolio compare with an S&P 500 buy-and-hold benchmark in terms of historical returns and risk?
4. What can quantitative risk models tell me about the potential weaknesses of my current portfolio allocation?

## Why I Chose My Own Portfolio

I chose to analyse my own portfolio rather than construct a hypothetical one because I wanted the results to be relevant to my investment decisions.

In particular, I wanted to understand where the risk in my portfolio came from and whether the findings would give me reasons to reconsider aspects of my investment strategy.

Using my own holdings also allowed me to investigate an investment allocation that I had already built, rather than choosing assets specifically for the purpose of the analysis.

## Project Scope

The analysis uses historical market prices to model the performance and risk of my current portfolio holdings.

The historical portfolio is simulated using current holding quantities rather than reconstructed from actual historical transactions. As a result, the findings describe the historical behaviour of the selected allocation and should not be interpreted as my realised investment performance.

The project focuses on measuring and comparing financial risk rather than predicting future prices or providing investment recommendations.

## Professional Objective

Alongside the financial analysis, I wanted this project to demonstrate my ability to use SQL and data analytics in a financial setting.

The project also provided an opportunity to apply mathematical and statistical concepts to financial risk modelling, while developing practical experience with data preparation, analysis and visualisation.
