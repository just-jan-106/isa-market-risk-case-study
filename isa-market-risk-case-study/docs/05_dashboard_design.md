# 05 SHARE — Tableau dashboard design

**Status:** Not designed or published.

## Audience and task
Target a non-technical portfolio owner/risk reviewer who needs a fast overview plus drill-down into the risk drivers.

## Dashboard wireframe / planned sections
1. **Overview:** portfolio risk summary, exposure, major assumptions and date window.
2. **Diversification:** asset weights, risk contributions, correlation matrix.
3. **Downside:** drawdown chart, VaR/ES estimates, rolling volatility.
4. **Scenarios / benchmark:** stress test and benchmark only if comparable data are credible.

## Chart choice and review
For each visual, record: audience question, metric, units, labels, chart type, limitations, source data, and whether its interpretation is supported.
